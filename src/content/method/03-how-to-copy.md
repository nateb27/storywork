## Read it first

Read the lesson, then read the passage once all the way through, aloud if you can. Don’t start copying until you know where it’s going.

## Copy exactly

Every word, every comma, every capital, the author’s spelling and paragraph breaks. If you make a mistake, cross it out with one line and keep going. No erasing.

## Copy slowly

Slower than feels necessary. Say each phrase in your head as you write it. The goal isn’t to finish; it’s to notice.

## Check it

When you’re done, compare your copy to the original, word by word. Circle anything you got wrong. Your mistakes show you where your ear expected something different, and that’s often the most useful part.

## A notebook

One notebook for all fifteen days, so you can see the work pile up. Lined or plain, it doesn’t matter. Paper you won’t be precious about.

## A pen you like

Something that moves easily. You’ll write for twenty minutes at a time.

## Thirty minutes

The same time each day if you can. Mornings work for most people, before the inbox does.

## Your phone, elsewhere

Open the day’s page, put your device on copy mode, and turn notifications off. Copy mode keeps the screen awake so you don’t have to touch it.

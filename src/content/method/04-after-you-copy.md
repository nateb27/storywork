Each day ends with a short question. Answer it by hand, directly under the passage you copied, in a few sentences.

## Why under the passage

So that a week from now you can see the passage and your thinking about it together, on one page.

## One day at a time

All fifteen days are open, but do one a day. The work is in the gap between days, when your ear keeps turning the sentences over without you asking it to.
